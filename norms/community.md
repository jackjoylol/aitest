# Hearthkeeper — community norms for the Pinewood Forest

> This file is your community's **constitution**. It is taught to your
> Minds steward at onboarding and re-affirmed on every review batch, so
> edit it to match YOUR community's real norms. Everything below is the
> default example used by the demo.

## Who we are

Pinewood Forest is a cozy art-sharing community for game artists. We
share WIP art, ask for feedback, and cheer each other on.

## Rules

1. **Be kind.** No personal attacks, name-calling, or harassment — even
   when disagreeing about art.
2. **No spam.** No crypto giveaways, referral links, or "DM me" schemes.
   One self-promotion post per week max, in #self-promo.
3. **Keep it on-topic.** Art talk in art channels. Memes are fine in
   #lounge, not in #critique.
4. **No hate speech or slurs.** Zero tolerance, instant removal.
5. **Keep it SFW.** No explicit, sexualized, or gore content. Artistic
   nudity in #mature-art with the trigger warning is tolerated.
6. **No doxxing or private info.** Never share someone's personal
   details, even if posted publicly elsewhere.

## How we enforce

- **allow** — fine as-is. Common case; don't over-police.
- **flag** — suspicious or borderline; a human mod takes a look.
- **remove** — clear violation; remove and log. First offense = warning
  DM, repeat offenses escalate: 2nd → 1-day mute, 3rd → 7-day ban.

## Ruled examples (precedents)

- "check out my new NFT collection, dm me for the link" → **remove** (spam)
- "your art is literally trash, uninstall" → **flag** (low-key harassment)
- "wip! trying painterly lighting" → **allow**
- "BUY NOW!!! LIMITED OFFER!!! 100x YOUR ART CAREER" → **remove** (spam/caps)
- "lol this looks like my 5yo drew it" → **flag** (negativity, borderline)
